Privacy

Night Shift Crane: Pier Seven can send anonymous gameplay statistics to an analytics service (such as ByteBrew, a CrazyGames analytics partner) when one is enabled on the page you play on, so we can see where the game is too hard or confusing and make it better.

What is sent: game events such as the game loading, the first input, a ship completed, an upgrade bought, a daily bonus collected, and how long a session lasted, with numbers like time played, ships, score and in-game money, plus the game version.

What is never sent: your name, email, CrazyGames account or user ID, chat, or anything you type.

Device ID: an analytics service may keep a random anonymous ID in your browser so sessions from the same device can be counted together. It is not linked to who you are.

Your progress (money, upgrades, records) is saved in your browser and, if you are signed in to CrazyGames, in your CrazyGames account so it follows you between devices.

Opting out: — the choice is saved in this browser and nothing more is sent. Clearing your browser data resets it.

Questions: contact us through the game's CrazyGames page.
